Codes for Packets
    1 - Join/Set
    2 - Receive ID
    3 - Broadcast Stats/Grid
    4 - Broadcast Block
    5 - Disconnect

Packets' structures
    1 - 1|username
    2 - 2|cID
    3 - 3|cID|GRID|STATS
    4 - 4|cID|ID_BLOCK|POSITION
    5 - 5|cID

Comando para executar o servidor:
g++ ../../src/network/clientData.cpp server.cpp main.cpp -lenet -o build/server.out
